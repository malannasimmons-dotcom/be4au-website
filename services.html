Services

Care rooted in your humanity — not a diagnosis, not a checkbox.

Be4Au is a clinician-led telehealth practice. Every service below is delivered by Malanna Simmons, LCSW — the practice owner, not a delegated associate — across Pennsylvania, Texas, New Jersey, and Michigan.

Tier 1 — General Outpatient

Accessible, high-quality care for everyday mental health needs.

🧭 Diagnostic Intake · CPT 90791

The Compass Session

"Let's figure out where you are and where you want to go."

A full 60-minute clinical evaluation — your history, your patterns, your strengths, and what's gotten in the way. You leave with a clear clinical picture and a collaborative plan, not just a diagnosis.

🌊 Individual Counseling · CPT 90834 / 90837

Foundation Therapy

"Consistent, grounded work on what matters most to you."

Standard 45–60 minute sessions for anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationship stress, grief, and general wellness. You see the same LCSW every time — no rotating associates.

🔥 Acute Support · CPT 90839 / 90840

Crisis Stabilization

"You don't have to navigate the worst moments alone."

Immediate, structured crisis intervention for acute distress. Every client has a documented safety plan and crisis protocol on file before it's ever needed.

👥 Group Therapy · CPT 90853

Collective Care

"Healing happens in community, not just in private."

Small telehealth groups (6 or fewer) facilitated by a licensed LCSW — clinically structured psychotherapy with real goals and progress tracking, not a support group.

🌐 Multi-State Continuity · All CPTs

The Portable Practice

"Your therapist should move with your life, not hold it still."

Licensed in PA, TX, NJ & MI — if you relocate or split time between states, your therapeutic relationship doesn't have to restart.

Tier 2 — Specialty & Premium

Advanced, specialization-driven care for complex clinical needs.

🧬 CPT 90791 / 90837

Rewired — Trauma-Focused Therapy

Trauma-informed CBT, CPT, and somatic awareness for complex PTSD, developmental trauma, and occupational trauma. Your responses made sense then — this work builds new pathways for now.

⚡ CPT 90791 / 90837

The Circuit — Neurodivergent Adult Therapy

Neurodivergent-affirming therapy for ADHD, autism spectrum, and twice-exceptional adults — including late-diagnosed clients. No compliance training, no deficit model.

💼 CPT 90791 / 90837

The Executive Session

Confidential care for executives and high performers managing burnout, imposter syndrome, and decision fatigue — with elevated discretion and scheduling built around you.

🔄 CPT 90837+

Recalibrate — Extended Sessions

75–90 minute sessions for trauma processing, grief, and identity work that benefits from more time and fewer context switches. Available standalone or as a session upgrade.
