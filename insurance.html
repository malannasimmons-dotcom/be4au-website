Policies

A few things worth knowing before your first session.

Late Cancellation / No-Show

A $50 fee applies to all clients, regardless of insurance or private-pay status, for cancellations under 24 hours' notice or missed sessions. This cannot be billed to insurance.

Sliding Scale

A limited sliding scale may be available for clients with demonstrated financial need who are not using insurance. Please inquire at intake — availability is not guaranteed and applies to Tier 1 only.

Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, uninsured or self-pay clients have the right to a written Good Faith Estimate before beginning services. Learn more at cms.gov/nosurprises.
