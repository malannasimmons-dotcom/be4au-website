FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly.

Is therapy only available by telehealth?

Yes. Be4Au is a 100% telehealth practice, which means you can meet from wherever you are — as long as you're physically located in Pennsylvania, Texas, New Jersey, or Michigan at the time of your session, per licensure requirements.

What if I move to a different state?

If you relocate between PA, TX, NJ, or MI, your care can continue without interruption since Malanna is licensed in all four. If you move outside those states, we'll talk through a plan for continuity of care and referrals.

Do you accept my insurance?

Be4Au is paneled with Headway, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare/Optum, and BCBS-affiliated plans across all four states. Visit the Insurance & Fees page for full details, or reach out directly to confirm your specific plan.

Do you accept Medicare or Medicaid?

Not yet — enrollment is currently in progress across all four states. If this applies to you, reach out to confirm current status before booking.

What happens in the first session?

Your first appointment is the Compass Session — a full 60-minute clinical evaluation covering your history, patterns, strengths, and goals. You leave with a clear clinical picture and a collaborative plan, not just a diagnosis.

What's your cancellation policy?

A $50 fee applies to cancellations made less than 24 hours before your session, or missed sessions, regardless of insurance or private-pay status. This fee is your direct responsibility and cannot be billed to insurance. See the Insurance & Fees page for full policy details.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

A limited sliding scale may be available for clients with demonstrated financial need who are not using insurance. Availability isn't guaranteed and depends on caseload capacity — please ask about it at intake.

What ages and populations do you work with?

Malanna works with adults navigating anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationship stress, addiction, trauma, and neurodivergence, along with specialty work for executives and high performers. See the Services page for the full menu.

Will I always see the same therapist?

Yes. You work directly with Malanna Simmons, LCSW — the licensed clinician and practice owner — at every tier of care. No rotating associates, no delegation.

How do I request records like a superbill or clinical letter?

Companion services like superbills, clinical letters, and coordination-of-care summaries can be requested through the client portal. Standard processing is 5–7 business days, with expedited requests available. See the Services page for the full list.

Still have questions?

Reach out directly — I'm happy to help you figure out if we're a good fit.
