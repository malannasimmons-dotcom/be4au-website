Meet your therapist

Malanna Simmons

"I'm Malanna — like Madonna, but make it therapy."

LCSW LMSW C-SSWS

I take an unorthodox, highly individualized approach to care because I believe mental health deserves the same sincerity, attention, and respect as physical health and wellness. I never take it lightly when someone sits in front of me and trusts me with their vulnerability. That kind of trust is meaningful, and I meet it with deep respect, care, and intention.

I'm big on collaboration. I believe the best work happens when your self-insight meets my clinical expertise. You already hold so much wisdom about your own life, even if stress, pain, trauma, or just life itself has made it harder to see. My role is to help you uncover the hidden strengths, buried wins, and natural gifts that may have gotten lost along the way.

I currently offer telehealth services, and I'm committed to making that experience feel just as connected, effective, and meaningful as sitting together in person. My goal is to create a space where you feel seen, supported, and able to do the kind of work that actually fits you.

I don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach to healing. Different things work for different people, and I believe that matters. I'm honored by the opportunity to help you figure out what works for you specifically, so you can move toward the mental, spiritual, and physical wellness that feels right for your life.

I also believe that every encounter can teach us something meaningful about ourselves — something that nudges us toward growth, even if only a little at a time. My skill set has allowed me to create care that is tailored to the individual, nurturing personal goals, honoring growth milestones, and supporting each person in a way that feels genuine and sustainable.

It will be a privilege to work with you.

Why Be4Au exists

Space over pressure. Guidance over silence. Support over expectation.

At Be4Au, I believe mental health deserves the same care, intention, and respect as physical health. I created this space with the understanding that healing is not one-size-fits-all, and neither is support. Every person comes with their own story, strengths, struggles, and goals, which means the care they receive should reflect that.

Be4Au was built to be a space where people feel supported, seen, and safe enough to grow at their own pace. Whether someone is navigating life transitions, relationship stress, addiction, emotional overwhelm, or the weight of simply trying to hold everything together, my goal is to offer care that feels human, effective, and aligned with who they are.

I do not believe in forcing people into rigid models of healing. I believe in meeting people where they are, honoring their vulnerability, and helping them move toward the mental, emotional, spiritual, and practical wellness they want for themselves.
